# Product vision

TAIYARI — Your Preparation. Your Future. A mobile-friendly AI-assisted study platform for Indian competitive government exams. Start with Railway RRB NTPC and Group D; expand to SSC, banking, state PSC, police and UPSC using configurable exam tracks rather than duplicated applications.

## Goals
Make daily preparation accessible, trustworthy and efficient. Connect current affairs to exam syllabi, practice mistakes to concepts, and performance to personalized revision. Build a daily habit with useful free content before deciding monetization. No pricing model has been approved.

## Learning experience
- Daily news digest: original concise English/Hindi summaries, date, source, category, exam relevance, archives and monthly revision.
- PYQs: verified source, exam/year/topic, options, solution, difficulty, bookmarks and mistake history. Never invent historical questions.
- Concepts: explanations, formulas, examples, shortcuts, common mistakes and related practice.
- AI tutor: grounded explanations, clear uncertainty, provider-independent backend.
- Mock tests: configurable exam pattern, timing, scoring, analytics and revision recommendations.
- User accounts, selected exam, study progress and personalized plans.

## Brand and distribution

Instagram is a complementary distribution channel for news, MCQs, PYQs and concept explainers; target 10 content units/day was discussed, not automated. Existing account rebrand is partial; verify handle and profile before publishing. No automatic public posts without approval. Reuse validated content across app and social channels.
